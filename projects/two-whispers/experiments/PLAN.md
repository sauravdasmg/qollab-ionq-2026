# Preregistered local evidence plan

## Hypotheses and scope

The receiver families are collective, textbook, and deferred sequential double-trine measurements. The default physical ensemble is two independently prepared copies of one equiprobable trine state, rotated by a seeded Haar SU(2) frame. The identity-frame mathematical anchor is case 0. Development uses the eight fixed seeds 2026100900–2026100907; frozen evaluation uses 2026101000–2026101023; independent fresh evaluation uses 2026101100–2026101123. Fresh manifests and sampling seeds are verifier-owned inputs. Inferences are conditional on ideal product-state preparation unless a source-dependent one-way bound is explicitly computed.

Primary claims on nominal local aria settings are (H1) the adjusted one-sided lower confidence bound for `e_seq - e_joint` exceeds 0.010, against the ideal one-way error reference `e_seq=0.0669872981`; and (H2) the adjusted lower bound for `0.9*e_textbook - e_joint` exceeds zero. Thus H2 requires at least 10% error reduction over the strongest budget-matched textbook receiver, not a tie. This is not a general LOCC/separable bound and says nothing about classical two-bit messages, which succeed perfectly.

## Baselines, budgets, and recipes

Compare all three families on the same generated instances, source labels, shots, channel regime, and sampling seeds where the simulator permits. The textbook receiver is the decisive equal-budget comparator; preserve the untuned mathematical receiver as an anchor. Development search grants each family exactly 128 objective/compiler evaluations per receiver family over the development campaign, with the same shot and noise allocation. Freeze the best budget-matched textbook, sequential and equivalent joint recipes, unused-outcome maps, and source assumptions before held-out sampling. Standard/binary controls are reported separately and never pooled with the primary ensemble. Each local block is simulated individually at width at most three; physical 21-qubit packing is a cloud/runtime concern.

## Estimands and uncertainty

The primary metric is paired word-error difference, averaged equally over receiver families' three labels within each generated instance and then equally over instances. Report per-instance paired outcomes and raw counts/probabilities. Use one-sided 97.5% bounds for each primary hypothesis (Bonferroni alpha .025 each), descriptive two-sided 95% intervals, paired instance permutation, and nested count bootstrap. Resample entire packed outcomes within a run to preserve cross-arm covariance; never treat blocks packed in one run as independent generated instances. Full campaigns use 200 shot draws per instance/regime. Point estimates alone never establish a threshold. Keep incomplete/failure cases visible; no automatic retries of remote attempts.

## Decision and kill rules

Gate G1 requires uncertainty-resolved operational payoff and at least 80% projected power for both tests from development/calibration before held-out runs. Fail G1 if either condition is absent. Kill the synthesis extension if server canonicalization removes the measured payoff. H1 or H2 failing on the preregistered held-out evaluation is reported as a negative result; do not retune on held-out or fresh data. A local emulator result is never labeled Qollab evidence.

## Resources, outputs, and checkpoints

Full suite paths are implemented but reserved for the orchestrator. `--quick` is development-only: at most two development frames, ideal and nominal aria, at most two draws and a tiny bounded search; it uses only `results/quick-*`. Full suite runs save `results/<suite>.json`, figures, config/version/machine/UTC metadata, all generation and sample seeds, parameters, recipes and search cost, raw counts/probabilities, hashes, paired results, uncertainty, shot/qubit/gate accounting, failures and negative decisions. Resume checkpoints are deterministic and retain their original campaign/config hash; config mismatch requires a new output. A successful quick check is implementation handoff, not independent science PASS.


## WP-W-04 synthesis implementation and orchestration

Run `python synthesis.py --budget 128 --cases 1 2 3 4 5 6 7 8 --output-dir results/development-selection-w04` outside a worker session. Each family receives 128 candidates, each scored on all eight development cases and all three source labels using exact calibrated nominal Aria probabilities. The score is mean success, with the earliest candidate winning ties. Collective and deferred-sequential candidates vary record relabeling, row phases and completion phases; textbook candidates keep the measurement fixed and vary standard/default decomposition and compiler seeds. All use optimization level 1 and the same final channel compiler. Every candidate/frame is checked for unitary residual and every ideal terminal probability after output relabeling, both to 1e-10.

The exclusive-write `recipe.json` freezes one common realization per family, settings, source assumption and selection rule; `search.json` preserves every candidate, score, exact checks, untuned reference, operation accounting, CPU/wall time and recipe hash. Use `--recipe results/development-selection-w04/recipe.json` for subsequent campaigns. Held-out/fresh campaigns reject incomplete development selections. G1 without a completed selection reports numerical margins but cannot certify H2. Tiny search tests are labeled incomplete, never substituted for the full 128-candidate selection. Prior broken results are preserved and require new evidence paths. No full search or campaign was run by WP-W-04; the requested five-draw G1 command is smoke evidence only.
