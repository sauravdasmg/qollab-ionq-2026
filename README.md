# Qollab × IonQ Hackathon 2026: three entries

Built during the hackathon build window (Oct 9, 7 PM ET – Oct 11, 7 PM ET, 2026). Theme: **Quantum Unlocked**.
This effort is supported by Qollab & IonQ.

| Entry | What it is | Folder | Demo video |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Entanglement Court** | Two quantum preparations give identical measurement bars; cross-examine them in other bases and certify which one is entangled, with recorded IonQ simulator data. | [`projects/entanglement-court`](projects/entanglement-court) | https://youtu.be/dVbXgx0EbbE |
| **The Noise Tax** | How much noise erases a quantum advantage? A noise slider over two quantum strategies, recorded IonQ simulator runs, and an honest report of the preregistered checks that were not met. | [`projects/noise-tax`](projects/noise-tax) | https://youtu.be/-vbi4atEZaM |
| **Eyes-Free Entanglement** | One measured GHZ parity fringe as sound, a screen-reader-ready text receipt and a chart, with recorded IonQ simulator data. | [`projects/eyes-free`](projects/eyes-free) | https://youtu.be/npBzjaA9mCc |

Each folder has a `README.md` (full write-up, reproduction, disclosure) and `PROJECT_PAGE.md` (the Qollab page text),
plus code, tests, saved results and the independent verification reports (`review/`).

- `uploads/*-web.zip`: the published Qollab JS projects; `uploads/<name>.zip`: the Python versions (slot runs).
- `projects/two-whispers` and `projects/never-lose-twice`: earlier build-window entries, retired and merged into
  The Noise Tax; kept because The Noise Tax cites their recorded runs and adapts their code.

**AI disclosure:** built with AI assistance (AgentMesh routing to OpenCode free models, OpenAI Codex models, and
Claude Code); video voiceovers use Microsoft neural text-to-speech. Details are in each entry's README.
